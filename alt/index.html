How do I book a court or register a contingent?
−
You can easily register through our portal or by clicking "Register Contingent". Choose your university, select your sports or cultural categories, and confirm your squad credentials.
Do I need a membership to play?
+
No prior membership is required! We welcome university contingents, independent athletes, and collegiate teams from across India to participate in official brackets.
Can I bring my own equipment?
+
Yes! You are encouraged to bring your own match-grade rackets, balls, and athletic equipment. Official tournament-grade equipment is also available at the sports complex.
What practice arenas and training facilities are available?
+
All registered university contingents have full access to warmup zones, practice courts, Olympic-grade track facilities, player locker rooms, and match equipment.
Are accommodation and meals provided for outstation teams?
+
Yes! Full outstation contingents receive on-campus residential hostel accommodation, dining hall passes, and round-trip campus shuttles upon registration.
What is your cancellation and refund policy?
+
Contingent registrations can be modified or roster substitutions made up to 7 days prior to tournament kickoff without administrative penalty.
